---
title: 'Reference review service'
description: 'What the Node.js service owns, and what remains outside its boundary.'
---

The draft reference service uses Node.js 24, Express 5 and PostgreSQL. It owns authenticated review, immutable bindings, trusted confirmation, claims and fresh admission, audit records, retention and advisory notifications. It is not a general executor.

Operator-managed directory entries distinguish producers, publishers and humans. A producer has verified ownership, permitted routes and an optional registered webhook destination. A publisher registers its own immutable App bundles and has no review-payload access.

The native inbox presents full retained documents, searchable payloads, response schemas, assignments and separate decision/audit/grant/execution states. The trusted response form remains available if an optional App fails. Request listing returns bounded pages; it does not load the entire tenant history into the browser.

Reads project expiry and withhold private material as soon as its captured retention deadline is reached, even if a cleanup job has not yet persisted that transition. Reads do not create audit events. The retention worker persists lifecycle changes separately with service attribution.

Authority-changing transactions serialise per tenant and sample PostgreSQL time after acquiring the lock. State, audit records, events and outbox jobs commit together. Notification delivery may retry, but it cannot confirm a response, authorise an action or move a deadline.

Execution grants require independent checkpoint acceptance. Permanent safety records fence generations, occurrences and identities across restore. The current production design has one active process per generation; restart requires offline recovery and fresh provisioning.

Use [configuration](/server/configuration), [authentication](/server/authentication), [deployment](/server/deployment) and [monitoring](/server/monitoring) before operating the service. Local fixtures and successful CI do not validate a production provider account or Plasm integration.
